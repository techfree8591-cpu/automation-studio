# Automation Studio Pro — Quick Start Guide for Customers

Congratulations on securing your **Automation Studio Pro & Viral Reels Autopilot License**!

Follow these 4 simple steps to have your channels posting on 100% autopilot:

---

### Step 1: Start the Studio
- **Option A (Desktop Only)**: Double-click **`Start_Automation_Studio.bat`** to open `http://localhost:4000`.
- **Option B (100% Free Android Mobile Access Anywhere)**:
  1. Double-click **`Start_Online_Studio.bat`**.
  2. It generates a free secure HTTPS URL (e.g. `https://xxxx.trycloudflare.com`) powered by high-speed Cloudflare CDN with zero setup and no IP challenges.
  3. Open that link on your **Android phone** anywhere in the world on 4G, 5G, or Wi-Fi!
  4. Select **Android** and enter PIN: `2009`.
  5. Tap Chrome menu (3 dots) → **"Add to Home screen"** to use it as a full-screen mobile app!

---

### Step 2: Connect Your Social Channels
1. In the **Account Connection & Verification** card:
   - Click **"Connect YouTube"** → Sign into your YouTube account and close the window when done.
   - Click **"Connect Instagram"** → Log into your Instagram account and close the window when done.
2. Click **"Refresh Status"** — you will see a green **Connected ✓** badge with your channel name!

---

### Step 3: Load Your Google Drive Viral Reels Bundle
1. Paste your Google Drive folder link into the **Download from Google Drive** field.
2. Click **"Download & Number"**.
3. All clips will download into your local queue and sequence automatically (`1.mp4`, `2.mp4`, `3.mp4`...).

---

### Step 4: Configure Schedule & AI Strategy
1. **Daily Frequency**: Select how many videos to post per day (1 to 5).
2. **AI API Key** *(Optional)*:
   - Click **⚙️ AI Keys** in the header.
   - Paste your free Google Gemini API key (or OpenAI / Claude key) so the AI Strategist can continuously research and write viral hooks tailored to your feed.
3. **Approve Copy**:
   - In the **Channel AI Strategist** chat, type *"Generate viral caption for video 1"*.
   - Click **"Approve & Apply"** to lock in your custom copy.

---

### Step 5: Autopilot or Instant Post
- To post immediately: Click **🚀 Post Now** in the top navigation.
- To run on schedule: Click **Save Schedule & Sync Windows** — your Windows Task Scheduler will publish automatically every day at your exact selected time slots!
